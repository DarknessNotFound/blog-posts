# Para — The Spirit With You — Convicting of Sin (1st Cor. 12:3)

Hello, please open your Bibles to 1 Corinthians 12:3. While y’all are doing that, I am going to introduce myself. I’m Grant Albright, I’m Brendan’s bass player and originally from Kansas City Missouri. However, as I’ve gotten to know Wisconsin’s beauty, I recently moved to La Crosse. Okay, that should be enough time, 1st Corinthians 12:3. 

 “Therefore”. Stop\! Whenever you read a “therefore”, “because”, “then”, etcetera, you should always stop and make sure you understand what’s happening before.

**Read** 1 Cor. 12:1-3a (ESV) – *Now concerning spiritual gifts, brothers, I do not want you to be uninformed. You know that when you were pagans you were led astray to mute idols, however you were led. Therefore I want you to understand…*

I love playing board games and, in my opinion, I’m really good at them. Who all was here last year? I was the guy that Brendan put up \$2 for anyone who could beat me in a board game.

During college, my friend Garren introduced me to this very complex board game and I got to play with some of his friends. Your goal in this game was to capture 3 cities alone or 4 cities with an ally. 2 to 3 hours into it, my ally and I had acquired 4 cities on round 4\. I was sooo stoked, I just won my first game\!

However, there was a catch. Garren was playing a faction that had to predict who was going to win and how fast they would win. If he was correct, then he won and everyone lost. Garren predicted that I was going to win in round 4\. I was devastated, he won and I lost\! Looking back, Garren manipulated and enticed me every step of the way so that I would win in round 4\. I thought I was so high and mighty but in reality, I was a lamb led to the slaughter.

Just like I was playing Dune, we are all easily led astray. All of us think we are so high and mighty but we are easily fooled. The world is going to do its best to lead you astray. We might not worship wooden trinkets anymore but we still worship idols. How many of us listen more to influencers on Tik Tok, Instagram, Facebook, Youtube versus the Word of God?

**Read** 1 Cor. 12:3 (ESV) *– Therefore I want you to understand that no one speaking in the Spirit of God ever says “Jesus is accursed\!” and no one can say “Jesus is Lord” except in the Holy Spirit.*

I want to explain some background about Corinth. Corinth was a major hub for sin during this time. Imagine taking Las Vegas, Los Angeles, Chicago, and Seattle and combining it into one, that's Corinth. Corinth had such a reputation, that the phrase “living like a Corinthian” was a way to reference someone’s hedonistic lifestyle to put it lightly. 

The Corinthians were surrounded by pagans and lots of idol worship. It was not uncommon for these ceremonies to include speaking in tongues. The difference between us and them is the source. We Christians speak from the Spirit of God, they spoke from demonic spirits.

**DQ:** Paul wanted the christian corinthians to discern those who speak in the Spirit of God versus those who speak in the spirit of the World. This applies to us today. I want everyone to gather in your groups and discuss what is meant by “speaking in the Spirit of God”.

**Give time for everyone to discuss (2 minutes)**

I’ll be honest, I had a really hard time finding a definition of this. My best understanding of speaking in the Spirit of God is when your speech is inspired by God. It can also be described as speech aligning with the Word of God. I would say this is supernatural speech given by God.

A great example of this is in the gospels is when Jesus asked his disciples, “Who do you say that I am?”. Peter answered, “you are the Christ, the Son of the living God\!”. Jesus praised Peter saying “blessed are you because flesh and blood didn’t reveal this but my Father who is in heaven did”. Peter was speaking in the Spirit of God.

‘…*no one speaking in the Spirit of God ever says “Jesus is accursed\!”...’*

You cannot be both for and against God. Anyone saying that Jesus is accursed is speaking from a demonic spirit, not the Holy Spirit.

“...*and no one can say “Jesus is Lord” except in the Holy Spirit.”*

The Holy Spirit has come into the world to testify about Jesus Christ. He draws us near and reveals to us that Jesus is Lord. It is only through the Holy Spirit that we can say “Jesus is Lord”. This wooing from the Holy Spirit is the *para*, or “with” relationship between us and the Holy Spirit. This is the pitcher next to the empty cup.

The *para* relationship is the first of the three relationships between us and the Holy Spirit. We went over this in the first session yesterday with the bucket of water analogy. Does anyone remember what the other two relationships are called?

**Give time for students to answer**

The other two are called the *en*, or “in” relationship and the *epi*, or “upon”, relationship. This is the bucket of water pouring into Val and then the pitcher overflowing onto Val respectively. I want to introduce one more connection between John 16 and the three relationships. Then I’ll briefly touch the *en* and *epi* relationship. Then I’ll spend the bulk of my teaching going in depth on the *para* relationship. Please open your Bibles to John 16:8-11

**Read** John 16:8-11 (ESV) – *And when he \[Holy Spirit\] comes, he will convict the world concerning sin and righteousness and judgment: concerning sin, because they do not believe in me; concerning righteousness, because I go to the Father, and you will see me no longer; concerning judgment, because the ruler of this world is judged.*

Concerning sin: this is the *para* relationship. The Holy Spirit convicts us of our sin and tells us that we need Jesus. I will go over this more in-depth during this session.

Concerning righteousness: this is the *en*, or “in”, relationship, where the Holy Spirit dwells within us. Remember, this is the bucket of water filling us. The Holy Spirit dwells in believers as a helper and sanctifier. As you grow in your walk with Christ, the Holy Spirit will sanctify you and make you more righteous over time. Josh will go in depth this afternoon.

Concerning judgment: this is the *epi*, or “upon”, relationship, where the Holy Spirit will come *upon* the believer and overflow as a power to service and witness Jesus Christ. This is the bucket of water overflowing Val. The Holy Spirit will overflow from a believer and give us the power to stand against our defeated enemy. Andy will go over this tonight.

Let’s go back to John 16 and break this down some more.

**Read** John 16:8 (ESV) – **“***And when he \[Holy Spirit\] comes, he will convict the world”* 

The greek word for convict here means a lot more than our simple English word. The original greek is a legal term that conveys ideas of exposure, refuting, and convincing. The Holy Spirit will expose our sin, refute our excuses, and convince us that Jesus is Lord. He does this for the whole world. The Holy Spirit convicts everyone.

**Read** John 16:9 (ESV) – *“concerning sin, because they do not believe in me;”*

When I was a wee little lad in the 4th grade, I remember having to take this really weird pop quiz. Normally, our tests are over math, history, or something academic. This test had 10 questions asking who your friends are, drawing a triangle, etc. However, the very last test question said “Ignore all the previous questions. Flip the page over, write your name, and put your pencil down”. 

I was very confused by this last question and then I read the directions. “Read all questions and then take the test”. I was duped\! I quickly erased all my previous answers, trying to act like I had actually followed the directions. It turns out, my teacher kept getting mad that we wouldn’t read directions so she decided to give us this pop quiz. I’ll be honest, it has been about 15 years and I’m still upset by this. If you need complex directions at the start of an exam, then you wrote a really bad exam. I do not need a page of directions to know how to circle C.

Our life is a series of test questions. Do I confess the truth or lie to get out of trouble? Do I do my homework and actually learn something or have AI do it? Do I forgive my friend or extract revenge and hold it over their head? The Holy Spirit tells us to read that last test question. Do you believe in Jesus Christ and pledge your allegiance to him? If you answer “yes”, then the whole test is thrown out. If you answer “no”, then you will be judged on the whole test. This is the only question that matters.

All of us sin and fall short of the glory of God. Our exam scores are in, we have failed. All of us deserve to go to Hell because of our rebellion against a holy God. However, Jesus died on a cross to pay our penalty so that we can spend eternity with God. If you just put your faith in Jesus and make him the Lord of your life, you can receive this free gift. 

This is the point Jesus is trying to make here. If you don’t believe in Jesus, then you are guilty of all your sins. If you believe in Jesus, then you are declared innocent. The only sin that matters is rejecting that Jesus is Lord.

“Okay Grant, cool beans, a lot of people reject Christianity and make claims against Jesus. How do I know this Jesus thing is legit?”. Good question\! There are a lot of arguments against God and against Christianity. Most arguments are just bad arguments. However, some are strong arguments that we can’t quickly dismiss. I can’t go over all these but if you have doubts about this whole Jesus thing, please ask the hard questions. Come talk to me or any of the leaders here. 

I do want to discuss one of these difficult propositions. What about the people who never got the chance to know about Jesus? Please go to Romans 1:18-23. 

**Read** Romans 1:18-23 (ESV) – “For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men, who by their unrighteousness suppress the truth. For what can be known about God is plain to them, because God has shown it to them. For his invisible attributes, namely, his eternal power and divine nature, have been clearly perceived, ever since the creation of the world, in the things that have been made. So they are without excuse. For although they knew God, they did not honor him as God or give thanks to him, but they became futile in their thinking, and their foolish hearts were darkened. Claiming to be wise, they became fools, and exchanged the glory of the immortal God for images resembling mortal man and birds and animals and creeping things.”

**DQ:** Everyone get back into your groups and discuss “How God is revealed through his creation?”.

God’s creation is amazing isn’t it? You can zoom really really tiny to the atomic level. All these little atoms build molecules that build cells. These cells are essentially tiny factories automated by the most complex coding language. These all combine to create organs and then full size animals or human beings. Then you can zoom all the way out to the scale of Earth and realize that we are a tiny spec compared to our sun. Going further, there are over 100 billion stars in the Milky Way. However, our Milky Way is one of 2 trillion estimated galaxies in the universe. God’s creation is such a large scale, we really can’t comprehend it.

My favorite argument for the existence of God is the fine tuning argument. I can’t go all the way into it but I’ll share a small amount. There are a lot of universal constants in physics that allow life to exist. For example, imagine you have a dial for gravity with 10 to the power of 40 notches, that is a 1 followed by 40 zeros. If you turn that dial by a single notch, then our sun wouldn’t exist.

To give you an idea about how precise this is, I’ll give you an illustration. Imagine taking every single grain of sand on Earth and putting them in one big pile. Take a single grain of sand, paint it blue, and then thoroughly mix it into the sand. Randomly grab one grain of sand. Now do this twice. Picking that blue grain of sand twice in a row is a similar chance as gravity being randomly chosen so that life could exist.

As you can see, God reveals himself in his creation and he doesn’t want any of us to perish. He is very patient with us. Imagine if God smited you every time you sinned? I know I wouldn’t be here. God is patient so that the Holy Spirit can draw us to repentance and we can be saved.

I know most of y’all are too young to start dating yet, however, I want to give some quick advice. Gentlemen, when you ask a girl out, be very clear that you want to go on a date. Also, only ask if you are okay with getting a “no” and be a gentleman about it. Ladies, if a guy asks you out, say a very clear yes or very clear no. Guys are simple but dense creatures that need a clear no. If you are wishy-washy then the guy will still think he has a chance, a clear “no” allows him to move on.

Our God is a gentleman, he will pursue all of us and draw us closer to him. However, being a gentleman, he will respect your rejection eventually. If you keep telling him “no”, he will stop asking and let you spend all of eternity separated from Him. This should be very scary for anyone who hasn’t given their lives to Christ. 

**Read** Matthew 12:31-32 – “Therefore I tell you, every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven people, but the blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven. And whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man will be forgiven, but whoever speaks against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven, either in this age or in the age to come.”

This is terrifying. There is one sin in which you will not be forgiven of; blasphemy against the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit knocks, and knocks, and knocks but will eventually say “fine then, your will be done”. At this point, God accepts your rejection of the Holy Spirit and lets you bear the full weight of your sin. Blasphemy against the Spirit is this final “no”.

Who remembers talking about Jezebel from last year? Who was she?

Ahab was described as “more wicked than all the rulers of Israel before him”. I would not recommend being \#1 on a list of wicked kings that God is angry at. They say behind every great man is a great woman. The great woman behind Ahab was Jezebel. Jezebel was exceedingly wicked and the mastermind of the operations (Ahab was very passive). She is a great picture of someone who has blasphemyed against the Holy Spirit. God has given her up to a debased mind as we read in Romans 1\. 

All of us have sinned and fallen short of the glory of God. We all can be like Ahab and Jezebel. Every leader here can attest to this fact. We like to play stupid games and act like we are good, upright people but if we’re honest, we all suck. We’re prideful. We lie, cheat, and steal to get ahead. We say nasty things to other people and about other people. Our base desire is to be God of our own life.

Fortunately for those who are saved, nothing can separate us from the love of God. If you are not saved, tread carefully. You can reject Him for a final time and bear the full penalty for sinning against a Holy and Righteous God. If you are worried that you might have committed this sin, this is a good thing. The fact that you worry means the Holy Spirit is still calling you to him.

However, you can stop worrying. I know you’ve done wicked things and you deserve to be punished. God already loves you enough to pay the penalty. The Holy Spirit is calling you today to give your life over to him. You can’t earn your salvation, you only have to put your faith in Jesus. If you accept this free gift, not only do you escape Hell, but you get to spend eternity with my loving Father and he becomes your loving Father.

If you have any questions, please ask them. I’m happy to talk to you. Your leader is happy to talk to you. This sermon was for those who aren’t believers yet and are still empty glasses. Next sermon we will be discussing the Holy Spirit filling believers upon salvation.
