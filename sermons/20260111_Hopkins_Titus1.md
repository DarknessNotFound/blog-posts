Introduction  
Paul will be graduating this May and then this church will be without a pastor. Finding and assessing a new Pastor is difficult work. Fortunately, we have two letters from Paul, the one that lived in Rome 2000 years ago, that includes specific instructions about finding men to lead a church. One letter was to Timothy and the other was to Titus. My plan is to preach through Titus whenever I’m filling in for Paul, the modern day one.

Transition  
We’ll start in Titus 1 which contains Paul’s instructions to Titus for strengthening every church in Crete. Crete had a bad reputation back in the day. This would be the equivalent of having to find high quality leaders for every church in Las Vegas, LA, San Francisco, etc. 

Roadmap  
If you haven’t already, open to Titus 1 where we’ll see how we, and our leaders, should be grounded in the truth, above reproach, and be free from legalism. 

*Pray*  
*Read Titus 1:1-16*

**Be grounded in the Truth**  
Statement  
As believers, one of our greatest weapons against the enemy is being grounded in the Truth.

Explanation  
Last time we discussed Genesis 3 and saw how Satan used Eve’s misunderstanding of God's law, don’t eat of the fruit of the tree of Good and Evil, against Eve to deceive her into giving into sin. 

Argumentation  
*Reread verse 1\.*   
Paul is talking about wanting us, the Church, to know the truth in godliness. There is a lot of truth in the world. 

- Who agrees that 2+2=4?   
- Who agrees that the sun rises in the East and sets in the West?   
- Who agrees that gravity exists?   
- Do any of these truths lead to godliness? Paul is discussing the truth that we are all sinners and the only path back to God is through our faith in Jesus, who died in our place and then rose again. 

Illustration  
If you’ve ever bought anything expensive at Walmart, you’ll notice that they have these security tags. These tags are too secure\! When I first started cashiering, I didn’t understand how these tags worked and I would fight them while a line of impatient customers built up. Eventually, a coworker would come up and take it off instantly. Between customers, I would mess with the security tags until I understood how they worked. Now I get to be the hero for the newer cashiers\!

Application  
Knowing the Truth keeps you on a path of godliness. People love to quote scripture out of context and use emotional manipulation to convince Christians to align with their ideology. Most of these lies are refuted by reading the Bible in context. 

Transition  
There are two ditches on the side of the road here. One ditch is legalism, which we’ll cover later, and the other is being above reproach. Let’s dig deeper into how a leader is supposed to be above reproach.

**Being Above Reproach**  
Statement  
Being above reproach making sure you are right with God (vertical), right with others (horizontal), and right with yourself.

Explanation  
This means that nobody should be able to look at your life and bring serious charges against you.

Argumentation  
*Read v6*  
There are three points here. He must be devoted to one woman: no side chicks, not leaving a trail of messy, unbiblical divorces, etc. He must have children who are believers: if he can’t lead his family, then he can’t be trusted to lead a church. He must not be open to the charge of debauchery or insubordination.
