**Strong & Steady; Actively Waiting**  
Statement  
Older men and women should reflect holy, reverent behavior that comes with age.

Explanation  
C.S. Lewis once said that “We are all slowly becoming either more heavenly creatures or more hellish creatures with every decision we make”. Compound these small decisions over the decades and you’ll see a huge effect. Folks

Argumentation  
*Titus 2:2* “*Older men are to be sober-minded, dignified, self-controlled*”. This is the image of a man who is a solid, honorable anchor for those around him. “*Sound in faith”* A man who has seen the faithfulness of God over many years. “*Sound in love”* A man who makes daily choices to put those around him above himself. “*And in steadfastness”* In Greek, the word is hupomone (who-pom-a-ney) indicating someone steadily striving for a goal. This is a man actively awaiting Christ’s return and trying to bring his Kingdom here on Earth.

*Titus 2:3a “Older women likewise are to be reverent in behavior, not slanderers or slaves to much wine.”* Ladies, note the “likewise” in that verse, you also need to be an honorable rock for those around you. Everything I just said an older man should be, applies to y’all also. Paul does specify gossip and then reiterates sobriety here. This was most likely a specific issue in Crete amongst the older women. However, this is a good reminder today.

Illustration  
Over the summer, I got to visit the National Redwood Forest that has these massive trees. To even see the top of them, you have to basically look straight up and hurt your neck. They are really beautiful and humbling to be around so I’d recommend visiting if you get the chance. 

Like these trees, I desire for you older folks to be a reverent people of God that humble youngin’s like me. 

Application  
I want to go back to “steadfastness” for a moment. Keep a steady stride for the faith. I’ve seen a lot of older folks, usually men, who retire and their health degrades dramatically within a year or so. A lack of purpose, which work provides people, quickly degrades your health. Fortunately, we Christians always have a purpose to glorify God and bring Christ’s Kingdom onto this Earth. If you’re not already, find ways to advance the Kingdom of God here in our corner of Missouri. Find a place to volunteer and fulfill practical needs. Find a part-time job where you can pour into your customers and coworkers. 

One example is the Southern Baptist Disaster Relief. This group goes around the country and helps with clean up efforts after disasters. When a tornado struck Rolla last spring, they showed up and did a lot of clean up. A lot of people have given their lives to Christ because of the natural conversations that happen while doing these clean up efforts. The Aldens who go to Laura Street have a chainsaw team here in Maryville. Additionally, there will be a training in April in Maryville for those interested. I’ve been a part of this organization while I was in college and I highly recommend them.

**Training the next generation**  
This is a popular concept in our culture\!

Statement  
Our world is confused about what a strong, Godly woman looks like.

Explanation  
Our world teaches young women to go wild, sleep around, then you can girl-boss your way into success. Husbands and children are a burden in which you don’t get paid. The young women of today need strong Godly women to teach them away from this nonsense. Let's break this down.

Argumentation  
*Self-controlled* – This is the Greek word *sōphrōn* (so-from) means to be in sound mind and to curb one’s desires. \**Pull out cell phone*\* Online shopping and social media lead to desires for other women’s bodies, their relationship, their house, etc. A lot of young women battle insecurities because of these comparisons and need to be taught their value in Christ. Other young women feel entitled to the world because of these comparisons and need to be taught to curb their desires.

*Pure* – Our world teaches young women that to be beautiful and powerful, you must be hyper-sexual. We should teach that you can be beautiful without being revealing and to save yourself for your husband. Additionally, a large amount of romance novels contain sexually explicit material and can be summed up as pornagraphy for women.

*Working At Home –* Managing a home is not an innate skill, this must be taught. We must be careful with this section. Both misogynist and feminist will abuse this verse. In context to the rest of the Bible, this is saying that a woman shouldn’t be idle at home. Our culture hates stay at home Moms. You can glorify God as a woman with or without a career. Regardless, young women need to be taught how to manage a home.

*Kind –* Being kind and graceful without compromising our Christian values is a difficult thread to weave. Being kind while being firm about God’s truth needs to be taught.

*Submissive to their own husbands –* Oh boy, our culture really hates this one. Submission is seen as a derogatory term to our American ears. However, submission is a natural, God honoring part of being a Christian. King Jesus submits to God the Father and his perfect obedience was displayed on the cross. We, the capital C Church, are called to submit to Jesus. We are called to submit to our government. We are called to submit to our church leaders. We are called to submit to our bosses. Submission isn’t an issue in God’s design, it was a part of the original design before sin entered the world.

The primary goal of marriage is to reflect the relationship between Jesus and the Capital C Church. Wives, your role is to submit to your husbands to show how the Church should submit to Jesus. Following Jesus, who is perfect, is difficult. Following your husband, who is a sinful, is difficult. God promised this would be a challenge for the wives all the way back in Genesis 3\. Submission is contrary to our human nature and younger women need older, godly women to teach them how to faithfully submit. 

There is a non-spiritual side to this as well. There will be times when you argue for weeks over an important decision that must be made. Since you said “death unto we part” then one must submit to the other.

Woah, similar to earlier, what I just said applies to the young men as well. Let’s go through this again.

Statement  
Like the women, young men are confused about how to be a man. 

Explanation  
One side of the culture basically tells young men to shut up, be docile, and apologize for your Y-Chromosome. The other side of the culture tells young men to be strong and capable so that you can exploit everyone and everything for your personal pleasure. We need strong, Godly men to teach younger men how to be strong and capable so that we can be a blessing to the women and children around us.

Argumentation  
*Self-controlled* – Young men have the tendency to do whatever is most pleasurable in the moment. They need to be taught how to be uncomfortable and sacrifice for those around them. There is a reason Paul reiterates this again for the young men.

*Pure* – \*Pull out cell phone\* With this black box, I can see more nude women than all of my ancestors combined have seen. Lots of young men need repentance and accountability from pornagraphy. 

*Working At Home –* Young men need to be taught how to assess and engage in the home-situation. This is especially important with a household with young kids. Being able to show up after work and turn on “dad mode” is a learned skill.

*Kind –* Exact same as the ladies: Being kind and graceful without compromising our Christian values is a difficult thread to weave. 

*Leading their wife –* Young men need to be taught about how to be head of their household. Culture tells men to either shut up and be docile or be domineering over the women in their lives. Men’s role in marriage is to reflect Christ’s love for the Church. He has a duty to do good for his family and not himself. He gets final say in decisions but, like Christ, must take on the hardest jobs. 

Statement  
The younger generation have the best opportunities to succeed but also have the most distractions.

Explanation  
Older generations, rightfully so, get frustrated with the youth for wasting their lives when they have soooo many more opportunities than they had. Today, we have instant access to all of human knowledge. You can “easily” start a business and have access to millions of customers using the internet. 

However, this little device has many distractions. 

* Previously, to see a woman naked, you had to go seek it out by magazine, a strip club, or convincing a woman to marry you. Now, anyone can get instant images from the comfort of their home.  
* Previously, to gamble, you had to physically go to a casino or go out and buy lottery tickets. Nowadays, there are thousands of ways to gamble. You can sports-bet, use online casinos, bet on world events, lootboxes in video games, cardboard cards, Labubu dolls, etcetera. All from the comfort of your home. Count how many times you see sports gambling ads today in the superbowl.  
* Watch any TV show or movie you want.  
* Listen to any song.  
* Play video games

And the list goes on and. Video games, TV, and music aren't even a bad thing but they can be a distraction. Paul emphasizes self-control in Titus. Cell phones are very addicting and the younger generation needs help with self-control around them.

**Our Lives can adorn the doctrine of God the Father**  
*Read v9-10*  
Statement  
No matter your lot in life, you can adorn the doctrine of God the Father. 

Explanation  
One of the lowest status you can have was being a slave. Paul is not endorsing slavery but telling slaves to act in a way that glorifies God. Regardless of your position in life, you can still glorify God by how you act.

Argumentation  
Paul is telling slaves to be submissive and well pleasing to their masters. Similar to husbands/wives, this doesn’t mean the slave has to sin for their master but should do their best work. Pilfering means to hold back a portion of a transaction and embezzle money from their owner. By being a hard, honest worker, a slave may adorn the doctrine of God our Savior.

Illustration  
When you walk into Walmart, how often are the workers joyful and hard working? My coworker Zack, who is a Christian, is very outgoing and engaging with customers. It amazes me how much his energy has a positive effect on people. Zack’s joyfulness is infectious and allows him to minister to people around him. His contrast to the rest of the cashiers gives him a chance to share the Gospel. 

Application  
Look around you for opportunities to bring joy into situations where there is usually conflict or depression. It is amazing how much of a difference you can make by being the stark contrast to those around you.
